P1 - AUTO-FARM OVERFLOW SHUTOFF          [most practical build in this file]
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Problem: totem farms keep producing after storage is full and items vanish.
Fix: sense the backup, cut the totems.

  [CONVEYOR SENSOR] --\
                       [AND] --> [TOTEM DISABLER]
  [SWITCH "armed"] ---/

  Place the Conveyor Sensor at the END of the output line, past your chests.
  Items only reach it when everything upstream is full. Its pulse plus the
  armed switch powers the Totem Disabler, which kills every totem within
  9.5 blocks until the line clears.

  Blocks: 1 Conveyor Sensor, 1 And Gate, 1 Switch, 1 Totem Disabler, 1 generator.
  Notes: the sensor pulse is SHORT, so the disabler blips rather than latching
  (no memory in this game - see rule 6). For sustained shutoff, place the sensor
  where items sit ON it continuously while backed up, not where they pass by.
  Requires L3 bench and Forging 36.

P2 - COMBINATION LOCK / VAULT DOOR
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  SW1 --\
         [AND]--\
  SW2 --/        [AND]--\
  SW3 -----------/       [XOR] --> [LED green] and/or [SPLIT CONVEYOR]
  SW4_decoy -------------/

  The AND chain requires SW1-3 all ON. The final XOR means SW4 must be OFF -
  flipping everything on breaks it. That is the only "must be off" condition
  available in the game (see cookbook sub-circuit 5).

  Blocks: 4 Switches, 2 And Gates, 1 Xor Gate, 1 LED, 1 generator = 9.
  Upgrade: replace the LED with a Split Conveyor to physically move a block out
  of a doorway, or a Totem Disabler to shut down a defensive setup.

P3 - ANALOG POWER METER / SOLAR SUNDIAL       [underrated - uses the analog side]
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Power is an amount, so a chain of splitters set to 90/10 makes a bar graph.

  [SOLAR PANEL] -> [SPL 90/10] -> 10% -> LED1
                        \-90%-> [SPL 90/10] -> 10% -> LED2
                                     \-90%-> [SPL 90/10] -> 10% -> LED3
                                                  \-90%-> LED4

  Each LED needs its tap to stay above the lighting threshold. As the Solar
  Panel's output rises with the sun (0 up to 26), taps cross that threshold one
  by one and LEDs light in sequence. Point it at a solar panel and you have a
  clock that reads out time of day. Point it at a coal generator and you have a
  fuel gauge.

  Blocks: 1 Solar Panel, 3-5 Splitters, 4-6 LEDs.
  Tuning: this is the one build that needs real in-game experimentation, since
  the LED minimum power is undocumented. Adjust splitter ratios until the bar
  spreads across the day nicely.

P4 - ITEM SORTER / RANDOMIZER
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Randomizer (wiki-confirmed trick):
  [GEN] -> [TIMER] -> [SPLIT CONVEYOR]
  Timer flips every 2s, so items alternate between straight and side output
  based on when they arrive. Effectively random for a busy line.

Every-item diverter:
  [CONVEYOR SENSOR] -> [SPLIT CONVEYOR]
  Sensor pulses when an item passes; the split conveyor kicks it sideways.
  Place the sensor one belt UPSTREAM of the split conveyor so the pulse lands
  while the item is on the splitter.

Gated sorter:
  [CONVEYOR SENSOR] --\
                       [AND] --> [SPLIT CONVEYOR]
  [SWITCH] ----------/
  Divert only while you have the switch armed. Manual sorting mode.

  Blocks: 1-2 Conveyor Sensors, 1 Timer or 1 And Gate + Switch, 1-2 Split
  Conveyors, 1 generator.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
P5 - ANIMATED LED SIGN / MARQUEE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  [STEAM GEN] -> [TIMER] -> splitter tree -> LED grid

  You get ONE 2-second clock (timers do not chain), so you cannot make a
  scrolling marquee in the classic sense. What you CAN do:
    - Half the grid on the timer, half on a plain generator = alternating
      two-frame animation. This is the whole trick.
    - Vary brightness per LED with splitter ratios (90/10 taps look dimmer).
    - Add a Button branch so a player press adds a third state for 2 seconds.

  Blocks: 1 Timer, 7 Splitters (for 8 LEDs, depth 3), 8-16 LEDs, 1-2 generators.
  Watch splitter depth: an 8-LED tree is already 3 deep, a 16-LED tree is 4.

P6 - AUTOMATIC FIREWORK SHOW
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  [STEAM GEN] -> [TIMER] -> [SPL] -> [SPL] -> 4x [FIREWORK LAUNCHER]

  One firework per power-on, 90 held per launcher, so 4 launchers = 360
  fireworks, firing 4 at a time every 2 seconds = about 3 minutes of show.

  Staggered finale trick: set the splitters to lopsided ratios (90/10). The
  starved branches sit near the firing threshold, so as the generator's output
  fluctuates they drop in and out while the strong branch fires steadily.

  Blocks: 3 Splitters, 1 Timer, 4 Firework Launchers, 1 Steam Generator.
  Cost warning: launchers are 100 Iron + 50 Steel Bolt EACH. 400 iron for four.
  Add a Switch between generator and timer so you can turn the show off.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
P7 - BASE SECURITY LIGHTING
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  [CONVEYOR SENSOR at entrance] --\
                                   [OR] --> [SPLITTER tree] --> LEDs + Fireworks
  [SWITCH manual panic] ---------/

  Run a short conveyor across your doorway. Anything that rides it trips the
  sensor and lights the base / launches a firework as an alarm. The OR gate
  gives you a manual trigger too.
  Blocks: 1 Conveyor Sensor, 1 Or Gate, 1 Switch, 3 Splitters, 4 LEDs,
          optional 1 Firework Launcher, 1 generator.
