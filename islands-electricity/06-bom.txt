================================================================================
PROJECT 04 - 4-BIT CALCULATOR (the big one)
================================================================================
Blocks:
   7 x Xor Gate ............ 140 Iron ...  280 Electrite
   7 x And Gate .............  70 Iron ...  105 Electrite
   3 x Or Gate ..............  30 Iron ...   45 Electrite
  14 x Splitter ............. 112 Iron ...  350 Electrite
   8 x Switch ...............  40 Iron ...  160 Electrite
   5 x LED Light ............  10 Iron ...   40 Electrite
   2 x Steam Generator ......   0 Iron ...  300 Electrite
                                            + 2 Gearbox, 40 Copper Bolt
  -----------------------------------------------------------------
  46 blocks total          TOTAL: 402 Iron Ingot, 1280 Electrite,
                                  2 Gearbox, 40 Copper Bolt

  Budget substitute: swap the 2 Steam Generators for 4 Coal Generators
  (160 Iron, 16 Electrite) - cheaper on electrite, worse on power headroom,
  and you will probably need to fiddle with splitter ratios.

  Per extra bit beyond 4 bits: 2 Xor, 2 And, 1 Or, 4 Splitter, 2 Switch, 1 LED
  = 106 Iron, 300 Electrite, 11 blocks. Consider one more generator per 2 bits.

================================================================================
SUGGESTED BUILD ORDER (cheap -> expensive, each teaches the next)
================================================================================
  1. Flasher      (timer + splitter + 2 LEDs)     - learn the wire tool
  2. P2 Lock      (9 blocks)                      - learn AND chains and XOR
  3. P3 Meter     (10 blocks)                     - learn analog splitter ratios
  4. P1 Shutoff   (5 blocks, needs L3+Forging 36) - actually useful
  5. Half adder   (4 blocks, cookbook #2)         - prove XOR+AND before scaling
  6. P04 Calculator (46 blocks)                   - the real project
